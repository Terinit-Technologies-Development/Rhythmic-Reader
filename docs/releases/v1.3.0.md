## Release summary

Reader remains the constructive outward-attention evidence authority.

- Routine preview Protocol 2 distinguishes CD3 daily-baseline targets from
  CD4+ provider-bound restorative gates, retaining its V1 compatibility path.
- Daily Evidence V2 remains the CD3 source: 3600 verified active seconds and 36
  qualified pages for the exact local date.
- Recovery Session V1 remains the CD4+ source: 1800 active seconds and 11
  qualified pages bound to the exact Routine gate/session ID.
- Daily Evidence and bound recovery evidence remain separate; neither silently
  satisfies the other.
- No hidden cumulative Reader debt is added for later CD4+ gates.
